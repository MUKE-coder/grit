gritframework.dev

Walk left to right. In Go it is money: an integer in minor units plus a currency, which is the correct way to hold money. By the time it reaches TypeScript somebody has written number, because that is the obvious translation. Now you have float arithmetic on an invoice. Nothing here fails to compile. Go compiles, TypeScript compiles, they simply disagree, and the disagreement reaches a customer as a wrong total.
