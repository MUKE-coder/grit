These are counted, not estimated: 74,532 lines of Go, TypeScript, config and SQL in one scaffolded project, lockfiles and dependencies excluded. About two and a half megabytes, which is roughly three quarters of a million output tokens. Say the third number last and let it sit. The money is the small part of this. The zero is the argument. If someone pushes on the cost, be honest: at current frontier output pricing that floor is tens of dollars, not hundreds, and that is cheap for two weeks of work. The objection is not the price. It is that you buy it again every project, you get a different thing each time, and nothing you learn on project five can reach projects one to four. Which is exactly D1, the copy-paste start, with a bill attached.
