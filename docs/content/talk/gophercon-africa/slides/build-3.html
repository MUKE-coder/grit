The seed is not theatre. Twenty-five thousand invoices is where you find out whether the list page works, and it is D9 from the first act closed with one command.

gritframework.dev

Four commands, and the middle ones are the ones to defend. A list page at ten rows tells you nothing: the sort is instant, there is one page, and the N+1 costs ten queries. At twenty-five thousand you find the missing index on the first click, in front of nobody. grit start brings up the API, both frontends and the infrastructure together, which is D2 gone.
