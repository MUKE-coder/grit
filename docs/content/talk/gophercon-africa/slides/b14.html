B14 · and there is a deadline attached

“Someone has asked us to delete their data.”

Export everything you hold about one person, then erase it without breaking the rows that reference them, and keep a record that you did.

gritframework.dev

Black, because this one has a legal clock on it and people underestimate it badly. Three jobs in one request. Find everything about one person across twenty tables. Remove it without orphaning the invoice that must still exist for the accountant. And prove afterwards that you did it, on a date. The third part is why this belongs beside the audit chain rather than in a support process. A deletion you cannot evidence is a deletion that did not happen, as far as a regulator is concerned.
