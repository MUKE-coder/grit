B9 · one deployment, two companies

“Can two of our clients use one system?”

And not one query that forgets which company it is for. The place you forget is the place the data leaks.

gritframework.dev

The commercial version of this question is "can we resell it", and the answer decides the business model, so it gets asked early. The engineering version is harder than it sounds, because isolation is not a feature you add. It is a property every single query has to have, including the ones in background jobs, exports and admin screens. One forgotten where clause, once, and one client sees another client's invoices.
