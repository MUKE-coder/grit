An invoice is not seven decisions. It is one decision with seven consequences, and six of them are mechanical.

gritframework.dev

The insight is not "generate code". Lots of tools generate code. The insight is that the unit of generation is the resource, because a resource is the thing that actually spans the stack. An invoice is a table, a model, a service, an endpoint, a type, a form and a row in a list. Those are not seven decisions. They are one decision with seven consequences.
