Thirty-one field types ship. Five in focus, the rest blurred on purpose, because nobody needs the list. Take money, because it is the one everyone gets wrong. Say float and you have shipped a rounding bug that will surface in a total months later. Say money once and the decision is correct in five places you never visit. belongs_to is the other one worth naming: one word, and you get the foreign key, the constraint, the preload, the nested type, and a searchable dropdown in the form.
