A question

How long between an empty folder and your first real line of code?

Not the first line you type. The first line that is actually about the thing you were hired to build.

gritframework.dev

Ask it, then wait. Actually wait. Let someone say a number. Whatever they say, the honest answer in the room is days, not hours. That gap is the subject of this talk. If nobody answers, say: "for me it used to be about four days, and I did it eleven times before I got annoyed enough to fix it."
