B7 · asked after something goes missing

“Who changed this?”

Not a timeline that anyone with database access can quietly edit. An append-only record of every write, and a way to prove it has not been touched.

The answer, with the commands  gritframework.dev/problems/b7

gritframework.dev

This is the question that arrives on a bad day, and the answer "we have a log" is not good enough, because a log in a table can be edited by anyone who can edit the table. The word that matters is evidence. A compliance reviewer is not asking whether you record changes. They are asking whether the record could have been changed. Hold this one. It comes back with a hash chain and a verify button.
