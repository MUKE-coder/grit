Do not read this slide. It is a reference, and the only reason it is in the deck is that somebody always photographs it. Say three things and move on. One: a relationship is a word, not a migration. belongs_to with no brackets infers the model from the field name, and a field pointing at its own model is a tree. Two: the flags on the bottom row change what kind of resource it is rather than what columns it has. owned-by scopes every query to the person who made the row. append-only means no update and no delete, enforced in three places. public adds a read-only surface for the internet. Three: every one of these is in the docs, and the link is on the slide.
