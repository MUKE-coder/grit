D9

Ten rows hide every bug you have.

Empty tables, and mail that goes somewhere you cannot read. Pagination, sorting, N+1 and the slow query are all invisible until real data arrives.

gritframework.dev

Two halves of the same problem: you cannot see your data because there is none, and you cannot see your email because it went to a provider. Everything in a list page works at ten rows. Sorting is instant, pagination has one page, and the N+1 costs ten queries instead of ten thousand. You find out at the same moment your client does. The fix is boring and nobody budgets for it: realistic seed data, and a place where sent mail lands that you can open.
