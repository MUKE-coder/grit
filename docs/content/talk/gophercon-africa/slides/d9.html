D9

You test on ten rows. The client has fifty thousand.

Writing a seeder that produces realistic volume is a day's work nobody budgets, so every list page is judged on hand-typed rows. Pagination, sorting, the N+1 query and the missing index all look fine until the real data lands. Development email has the same hole: it goes to a provider you cannot open.

gritframework.dev

This one is about seed data, and say that word early or the room will not follow you. Everything in a list page works at ten rows. Sorting is instant, pagination has one page, and the N+1 costs ten queries instead of ten thousand. You find out at the same moment your client does. The fix is boring and nobody budgets for it: realistic seed data, and a place where sent mail lands that you can open.
