B8 · ten megabytes, sideways

“Our staff photograph things on their phones.”

Rotated, ten megabytes each, with the GPS of a client site in the EXIF. Uploaded over a connection that drops.

gritframework.dev

Nobody asks this as a question, which is why it is always a surprise. It arrives as a bug report: the app is slow, and the photos are the wrong way round. Three separate problems in one upload. Size, because nobody will wait for ten megabytes to load in a list. Orientation, because a phone stores the rotation in metadata and a naive resize throws it away. And privacy, because you are now storing the coordinates of a client's house. Fourth problem, and the only one that is an attack: an image can be small on disk and enormous once decoded.
