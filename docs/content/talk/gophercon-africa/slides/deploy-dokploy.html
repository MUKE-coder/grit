Two of the three settings have a trap behind them, so say them rather than clicking past. Compose rather than Stack, because Stack targets Swarm and does not support the build key. And the one that costs people an evening: Dokploy writes your dashboard variables into a .env beside the compose file rather than injecting them into containers. They reach a container through Compose substitution, so keep the ${VAR} in every environment block or add env_file. Skipping it is why a stack boots with an empty DATABASE_URL and a dashboard full of values.
