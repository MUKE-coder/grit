B1 · the first thing anyone asks

“Can different people see different things?”

Roles, then permissions, then per-record ownership. It is always one layer deeper than the first conversation suggests.

gritframework.dev

Say the quote, then the deepening. Roles is easy. Permissions is fine. Per-record ownership, where a salesperson sees only their own accounts, is where it turns into a week. And it has to hold in the background job and the export, not just the HTTP route, which is the part people get wrong.
