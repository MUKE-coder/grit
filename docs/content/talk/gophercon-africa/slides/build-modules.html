Four tables and three relationships. Small enough to build in front of you, and real enough that every one of the twenty problems shows up in it.

gritframework.dev

Introduce the domain in one sentence: an agency bills clients for projects, and some of those projects bill every month. Point at the chain: a payment belongs to an invoice, which belongs to a project, which belongs to a client. Three belongs_to fields, and each one is a word in a command rather than a migration and a form.
